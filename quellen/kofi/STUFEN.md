## Reader — 1,99 €/Monat
For students, or anyone reading on a small budget. You help keep Fichte, Schelling and Hegel open to everyone: no paywall, no account, no ads.

## Fellow — 3,99 €/Monat
For doctoral and postdoctoral researchers who work with the texts. Your support goes into checking the editions against the printed originals, page by page.

## Scholar — 5,99 €/Monat
For professors and established scholars who rely on the editions in research and teaching. You help bring out new volumes and keep the whole edition open for your students and colleagues.

Für alle drei: Mitglieder werden auf Wunsch unter den Friends of the Edition genannt. Die Mitgliedschaft schaltet nichts frei; Lesen, Suchen und die Daten bleiben für alle gleich.

About you:
I edit Fichte, Schelling and Hegel as free digital editions: more than forty volumes, each checked against the printed original and all searchable together at idealismedition.com. No paywall, no account, no ads, and it will stay that way.

A membership keeps the work going: Reader for students, Fellow for doctoral and postdoctoral researchers, Scholar for professors. Members can have their name inscribed in the Geisterreich, the list of Friends of the Edition at hegeledition.com/friends. Tell me in the checkout message how you would like your name to appear.
