# Hegel glossary: supplement to draft 1 (Nachtrag, 9 Nov 2026)

Companion to `hegel-glossar.md` (draft 1). Same corpus (16 GW volumes), same principles (section 0 of draft 1), same
columns. Decisions D1–D15 are **not** re-decided here; section 2 only adds evidence. New open points are numbered
N1–N9 (section 3). Everything else in section 4 is a default unless the owner objects.

**Corpus caveat found while checking (important for the early writings).** The portal's GW 1 ends with the Bern
fragments (Text 39, "Der Streit über …"). *Der Geist des Christentums und sein Schicksal*, the *Liebe* fragment and the
*Systemfragment von 1800* are in GW 2, which is **not** in the corpus: *Pleroma* has 0 hits, *Schicksal* 144 hits (mostly
PhG, history, Jena), *Liebe* 492 (mostly sermons, Volksreligion fragments, Jena, Religion lectures). Knox's *Early
Theological Writings* therefore covers only the 1795/96 *Positivity* essay (Knox Part I = GW 1, Text 32; Knox Part III,
the revised introduction of 1800, = GW 2).

### A′. Particles, adverbs, connectives

| German | n | English | Established | Danger / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| schlechthin | 1 033 | absolutely; simply (= without qualification) | MSL, dG (*absolutely*, *simply*) | Modern German "downright, plainly". In Hegel (and Fichte) it is a logical qualifier: *schlechthin ein Unmittelbares* (GW 11, 33) = "absolutely an immediate". Never "plainly", "utterly". Coordinate with the Fichte glossary. |
| überhaupt | 3 416 | in general; as such; at all (only under negation or in questions) | dG, BD | MT defaults to "at all". *Bestimmtheit überhaupt* = "determinateness as such / in general", *überhaupt nicht* = "not at all". Before a noun phrase it usually marks the most general form of a category. |
| eben, eben darum, ebendadurch | 3 291 | precisely, just; for that very reason; precisely thereby | | Not "even", not "flat". *eben weil es der Anfang ist* (GW 11, 36) = "precisely because it is the beginning". |
| ebenso, ebensosehr … als | 2 705 / 194 | likewise; just as much … as | | The balancing formula of the dialectic: *ebensosehr nur ein äusseres* (GW 12, 29) = "just as much merely an outer". MT gives "also very much": the symmetry claim is lost. |
| erst | 1 367 | only (then), not until; first | | "*ist erst* das Resultat" = "is only (now) the result", not "is first the result". Temporal-logical "only" is the default in the Logic. |
| zunächst | 1 335 | initially, at first, in the first instance | | Never "next". It marks the first, still immediate form of a category, which will be revised. |
| zugleich | 2 854 | at the same time | | Mostly logical simultaneity (two determinations hold together), not temporal. Do not render "also" (the claim of co-presence disappears). |
| vielmehr | 1 658 | rather; on the contrary | | Never "much more". Signals the dialectical correction of a preceding claim; keep it at the head of the clause. |
| insofern (als) | 1 834 | insofar as; to the extent that | | Correlative *insofern … insofern* (22 lines with both in one sentence span) = "insofar as …, to that extent …" (see traps, T7). |

| somit, hiemit, hiermit | 1 681 | thus, hence; thereby | | *hiemit* is the period spelling of *hiermit* and means "thereby / hence", not "herewith" (letter idiom). |

| gleichsam | 237 | as it were | | Hedges a metaphor; dropping it turns Hegel's simile into a claim. |

## 7. Sources, method, limits

**Checked (✓)** against full texts or glossaries: P (local PDF), dG (local PDF, translator's note pp. lxiii–lxxiv
read in full), BD (local PDF; glossary in the local epub), I (local PDF), MPN (local PDF), Pe (local PDF), MSL
(archive.org full text), CH (archive.org full text, Translators' Preface and Note on Conventions), K (archive.org
full text), HK (notes only, via marxists.org), FD (publisher's contents page), Knox 1964 and Nisbet 1999 (library
catalog contents pages). Counts in translations are regex counts on the extracted text (hyphenation joined): rough,
but they settle which word a translator uses.

**Not checked / not accessible:** Miller PhG (1977); Harris/Cerf *Difference* (archive.org lending copy, text
blocked); Harris/Knox body text; Knox *Natural Law* (1975) body text; Burbidge/di Giovanni (1986); Rauch (1983);
Hodgson LPR and *Proofs*; Nisbet *Reason in History*; Knox/Miller *Introduction to the History of Philosophy*;
Wallace (Logic; Philosophy of Mind 1894: available on archive.org but not examined); Geraets/Suchting/Harris (only
via dG); Brown, *Lectures on the History of Philosophy 1825–6* vol. II (local PDF is an image scan without text layer,
and in any case transcripts are outside the corpus); Inwood, *A Hegel Dictionary*; NDPR/Hegel Bulletin reviews (only
bibliographic data reached). Every attribution to these is marked (unchecked).

**Counting tool.** `zaehle.py count` builds `r'\b' + pattern`; with an alternation (`a|b`) the word boundary applies
only to the first alternative, so later alternatives also match inside words (e.g. `landstände|stände` also counted
*Gegenstände, Umstände*: 1 500 instead of 496). All counts in this supplement were made with the pattern wrapped as
`\b(?:a|b)`. Draft 1 counts that used alternations may be inflated; worth re-running with `(?:…)`.
