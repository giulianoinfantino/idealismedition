What data are processed when you visit this website, and whom to contact with questions.

2. Access data

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc. When it is accessed, technically necessary server log data may be processed there, in particular the IP address, date and time of access, browser type, operating system, and requested file. The purpose is to provide the website and keep it stable and secure; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, DSGVO).

3. No cookies, cookieless audience measurement

This website sets no cookies. Audience reach is measured by Vercel Web Analytics without cookies and without a persistent identifier: page views yield daily counts; a visitor is not recognized beyond the day. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in audience statistics (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The home page shows visitors per day over the last 30 days. Fonts and search data are loaded by this website itself, not by third parties.

Only your color scheme and your most recent search queries are stored in your browser’s local storage. Both remain on your device and are not transmitted. The search runs entirely in your browser: your queries are not sent to the server. Since this storage is strictly necessary for the service you have requested, it does not require consent (§ 25(2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG). You can delete this information at any time through your browser settings.

4. Feedback

You can write to us through the “Feedback” link at the foot of every page and “Report an error” in the reader. Data is transmitted only when you click “Send”: your message, an optional email address for a reply, the page and, where applicable, the passage your message refers to, and the language of the page; no IP address and no identifier.

The message is passed through a function of the website www.idealismedition.com (operated by Vercel Inc.) to GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA) and stored there as an entry in a non-public repository that only the controller can view. GitHub participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The data serve solely to handle your report and are deleted once it has been dealt with, at the latest after two years. The legal basis is your consent by sending (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); you can request deletion at any time.

5. Ko-fi payment panel (only after a click)

On the “Support” page, the Ko-fi payment panel (Ko-fi Labs Limited, United Kingdom) can be loaded into the page. This happens only when you click “Load the Ko-fi payment panel”; before that, nothing is loaded from Ko-fi. After the click, your browser retrieves content from Ko-fi’s servers. Ko-fi thereby receives your IP address and technical information about your browser and may set cookies. Ko-fi processes payments through payment providers such as PayPal and Stripe; Ko-fi’s privacy policy applies to this. The legal basis is your consent given by the click (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG); it applies only to this page view.

6. Links to other websites

The search hits and the volume titles lead to the editions at www.fichteedition.com, schellingedition.com, and hegeledition.com; their privacy notices apply there. For membership, this website links to Ko-fi. These are simple links: you leave this website only when you click one.

7. Your rights

Within the limits of the statutory provisions, you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, and restriction of processing, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Please send questions to giulianoinfantino[at]icloud.com.
