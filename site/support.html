Support

Become a member

The editions are freely accessible, without a paywall and without an account, and they are meant to stay that way. Anyone who wants to help sustain the work can become a member.

  • Reader

    1.99 € per month

    For students and anyone reading on a small budget.

    Become a Reader

  • Fellow

    3.99 € per month

    For doctoral students and postdocs who work with the texts.

    Become a Fellow

  • Scholar

    5.99 € per month

    For professors who use the editions in research and teaching.

    Become a Scholar

The tiers differ only in amount; choose what suits you. Payment goes through Ko-fi, right here on this page, and you can end your membership at any time.

Clicking a tier loads the Ko-fi payment panel; only then is data sent to Ko-fi (see Privacy).

If the panel stays empty, Ko-fi does not allow embedding in your browser: then go directly to Ko-fi.

What membership is

A voluntary contribution to the work on the editions. It unlocks nothing that is not already open: reading, searching, and the data remain the same for everyone.

What it pays for

Further checking of the texts against the print, and new volumes.

Friends of the Edition

Members who wish are named among the Friends of the Edition.
