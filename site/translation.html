Translation

Which English word?

We are translating Fichte, Schelling and Hegel into English, about six million words. Before a single page is translated, every key term gets one fixed English word, the same in all three authors unless the sense really differs. Most of these are settled by what scholarship has worked out over decades. The questions below are the ones that are not. I would like to decide them with you.

How it works

  1. Pick a rendering and say why. Every vote needs a reason. A passage from the text that breaks a rendering is worth more than any opinion.
  2. Mark the reasons that convince you. Each vote counts one point, and each time someone marks a reason convincing, its rendering gains another. So the best-argued rendering wins, not the loudest.
  3. Propose your own. If none of the options is right, add yours; others can then vote for it.
  4. One round, then it closes. The round closes on 30 November 2026. After that I publish every decision with its reasons, and the names of those whose arguments shaped it, if they want to be named.

To replace our draft, a rendering needs at least five reasoned votes. Where results contradict each other or the texts, I decide and give my reason. The questions with the fewest votes so far come first, so every term gets its turn.

No account needed. Your browser remembers your votes; we store only the option, your reason and the time. A rendering marked current proposal is our draft, not a decision.
